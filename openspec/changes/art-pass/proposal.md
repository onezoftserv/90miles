# Proposal

## Why

Everything in the world is still drawn with flat rectangles and circles: `DevIslandTerrain.draw` paints one colour per 16 px cell (and magenta for 8 of the 15 terrains), palms, fire, workbench, rain catcher, build site, raft, signs, drink spots, every pick-up (one tan 6×6 square) and every creature (a 5 px circle with a facing line) are `_draw` code, the crossing is one blue rect with scrolling lines, and every player is the same unanimated 16×16 knight that never shows crouch, swim or downed. On 2026-11-08 the owner settled the art: direction C, "GBA tropic" (a bright *A Link to the Past* / Pokémon look, 1 px dark outlines, 3/4 view), 16×16 tiles, players about 16×28 in 32×32 frames standing on one 16 px tile, built from packs downloaded with permission plus hand-made pieces kitbashed to one master palette, rough first. This change is the first pass that makes the game look like that end to end.

## What Changes

- **Asset pipeline.** Only the files actually used are copied from the git-ignored downloads (`game/assets/oga/`, `game/assets/itch/`, `game/assets/kenney/`) into tracked folders under `game/assets/art/<area>/`, each with a `License.txt` naming every source's licence and page; nothing tracked in the Godot project refers to the git-ignored folders. `game/assets/CREDITS.md` lists every pack used, and a new in-game credits screen (*Créditos*, from the title screen) shows every CC-BY credit with its licence address and source page: DawnLike (DragonDePlatino, palette by DawnBringer), Tiny, Tiny Heroes – Animals (Kacper Woźniak) and Kyrise's 16×16 RPG Icon Pack (Kyrise), plus courtesy lines for the CC0 packs. `tools/fetch_assets.py` learns all nine downloaded packs (page, licence wording, folder, archive type) and re-fetches what can be scripted, printing a manual step for the rest. One master palette (`game/assets/art/palette/`), an outline (rim) step and a palette check run in the pipeline (`tools/art/`, which reads the packs from a `--packs` root), and every tracked PNG imports lossless with no mipmaps.
- **Terrain.** The island draws its terrain as tile layers built from its `GridTerrain` on every peer, for every layout (the sandbar and the coast cay): land as corner-matched autotiles with transparent water sides (sand, dune and brush layers from the ArMM1998 Zelda-like set, recoloured, their baked water and foam keyed out, with the islet and diagonal tiles drawn by hand), a scrolling sea under them whose shallows are a lighter palette step than deep water, one animated foam line along every shore (Eldiran's 8-frame shoreline, recoloured, on the land side of the shore as DCSS does), tide pools and contact shade as decals. Every land terrain gets a tile and every water terrain a sea step, so the magenta fallback goes; the tile layers block nothing. Cosmetic variants come from a hash of the layout and the cell, never from a simulation RNG.
- **Players.** The player is drawn from the MetroCity CC0 base in 32×32 frames on one fixed feet line, anchored bottom-centre on the body with the collision shape unchanged, in three facings (down, up, side mirrored) taken on every peer from the motion of the drawn body: walk and run, crouch, swim cut at the water line, dive, downed and dead, all from the movement, stealth and downed states the host already replicates. The crouch pose replaces inventory-hud's squashed crouch cue. Each player gets one of four clothing colours through one player shader that also cuts swimmers at the water line; the host gives each body the lowest free look slot in its spawn data (falling back to a dropped body's slot when all four are held), a rejoiner keeps it, and a solo player is slot 0. The name tag, the quick-chat bubble and the HUD marks follow a per-pose head anchor.
- **Creatures and items.** The reef shark (a dorsal fin above the surface over a dark body cut at the water line, the crossing's passing shark too), the land crab and the seagull (Tiny, Tiny Heroes gull: perched in its palm's crown, flying with a lifted shadow) get frames in their `CreatureData.sprite_frames`; a creature plays the animation named after its brain's state, so a new creature with frames named after its states needs no script, and it counts as moving over a window longer than the client's 10 Hz position sync. All fourteen items (the sandbar's ten and the coast cay's nails, mirror, aloe and lighter) get a 16×16 `ItemData.sprite`, drawn on the ground (raised, with a shadow, hidden while another capability conceals the pick-up) and in inventory-hud's strip.
- **Structures.** Palm (climbable look with 0–3 coconuts shown and the gull nest on the layout's nest palms), a palm-frond lean-to standing at the back of the sandbar's hut, workbench, fire (lit, out, embers), rain catcher, island signs, the raft build site in three stages and the raft with integrity damage states (one 64×32 raft used on the beach, beached rafts included, and as the crossing deck). The lagoon's drink spots draw nothing of their own.
- **Crossing.** The crossing draws the same sea in its open-water step, scrolling with the crossing, the raft deck by integrity, and swimmers cut at the water line, with the sea and deck kept under every rider and the passing shark.
- **Depth and readability.** Actors, items, palms and structures are Y-sorted by their foot point without moving any node, so a palm canopy or lean-to roof covers whoever stands behind it; every actor and item has a drop shadow; measurable rules for actor-to-ground contrast, outlines, icon silhouettes and frame budgets are checked by tests.
- **Visuals never change simulation.** Art is client-side drawing: no art node writes simulation state, art adds no replicated property, and a scripted host run gives the same state with the art present or stripped.
- **Docs.** `CLAUDE.md` (the "16×16 tiles and 16×16 characters" line), `docs/ASSETS.md` (direction, packs, style guide), `HANDOFF.md` decisions, `game/assets/CREDITS.md` and `TASKS.md` record the owner's decision.
- **Later (not in this change):** the NPC cast and its extra bodies (child, elderly, pregnant, splint), dialogue, background and codex portraits, the `player-looks` change (owner decision, 2026-11-08: each background sets the outfit and one signature item drawn on the body; the player picks man or woman, skin, hair and clothing colour in the lobby, or on a title-screen screen in solo; extra hairs, hats and outfits unlock when found in a run and are remembered per install, the secret cay's red-band hat among them), every vessel beyond the raft (boats, almendrón, board, turtle, dolphins, patrol boats, the cutter), set pieces and buildings (lighthouse, bunker, the coast cay's watch post, wrecks, the piling, the resort), full autotile sets for mangrove, rock, cliff, shingle, reef, coral, hammock, casuarina and wreck interiors (this pass gives them a plain tile), weather particles (rain, squall, fog, lightning, waterspout, sargassum), the remaining player poses (own crawl frames, drag, carry, being dragged, climb, work, pick up, eat and drink, tied down, falling in, pulled out), held-tool hand anchors, the gull perched on a head, status cues on the body, HUD meter and status icons (Shikashi, after a palette reduction), map and POI icons, the title logo, the remembered-cell tint (needs field of view), item state badges, the other bestiary creatures, interpolated creature motion on clients, and polish (breathing idles, more frames).

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `art-pass`: how the game is drawn in direction C: tracked art with licences and credits, the master palette, outline and palette-swap rules, terrain tile layers with the shoreline overlay and the scrolling sea, player, creature, item and structure art driven by data and replicated state, Y-sort, shadows, the crossing's look, readability and frame-budget rules, and the guarantee that drawing never changes the simulation.

### Modified Capabilities
- `build-verification`: "No dependency on downloaded assets" now covers all four git-ignored download folders (`game/assets/kenney/`, `game/assets/oga/`, `game/assets/itch/`, `game/assets/audio/ambience/`) and adds that no tracked scene, resource, GDScript or shader under `game/` refers to a file inside them.
- `inventory-hud`: "Crouch cue" draws the crouch pose instead of the 10/16 squash, with the hidden mark over the crouch head anchor.
- `ui-shell`: "Title screen" gains *Créditos* before *Salir*, with its description in the focus panel.
