## 1. Summary for the owner

1. Both things you saw are real, and both come from how the beach tiles are built, not from the drawing order.
2. The bushes on the waterline are sliced on purpose by the tile builder (an old fix to stop bushes hanging over the sea); the fix is to place a whole, slightly smaller bush on those cells instead of cutting a big one.
3. Coasts look square because every corner piece of the sand is a right-angle block or notch; rounding those eight pieces rounds every coast, its foam and its shadow at once.
4. The edge of the deep water copies the coast one cell further out; a small change to the sea drawing turns it into soft curves.
5. The coast cay is drawn as rectangles in its map, so it needs its own reshaping change; the sandbar's map is a good circle and only needs the art fix.
6. Other things that read square: the swimmer's flat water line, the shark fin sitting on the raft, the flat grey rock, and the hard-cornered HUD boxes.
7. The beach and the shallow water are equally bright, so the coast only reads by colour; darkening the shallows one step fixes that.
8. At night the fire gives no light, and a squall shows no rain; both are cheap, drawing-only additions.
9. The HUD and menus use a different look and colours from the world; one rounded GBA-style window kit in the game's own palette fixes most of it.
10. Suggested order: whole shore bushes, rounded coast corners, soft deep edge, then the HUD windows; the choices that need you are at the end.

## 6. Decisions for the owner (recommended option first)

- **Q1. Coast corners: what shape should the outer corners take?** (a) Rounded arcs: soft beaches, convex tips keep a small hand-drawn bush (recommended). (b) 45° cuts: straight diagonal coasts, but tips lose most of their sand and their bushes, and lone corners look octagonal. (c) Arcs now, plus extra slope pieces later for perfectly straight diagonal stairs (C5).
- **Q2. Shore bushes: one fitted bush everywhere for now?** (a) Yes: one smaller whole bush now, 2-3 shore variants later in their own change (recommended). (b) Hand-draw 2-3 shore bushes before shipping the fix (slower, needs a spec change). (c) Keep today's cut.
- **Q3. Shallow water: darken it one step so the beach stands out?** (a) Yes, darker shallows plus a visible wet-sand edge (recommended). (b) Keep the colours, only uncover the wet-sand edge. (c) Keep as is.
- **Q4. Deep-water edge: art only, or also change the maps?** (a) Art only: soft curves from the sea drawing (recommended). (b) Also vary the width of the shallow ring in the maps (changes where you wade and swim).
- **Q5. Reshape the coast cay's rectangles?** (a) Yes, after the coast-cay-bench work merges (recommended). (b) Later. (c) No.
- **Q6. Swimmer's water line?** (a) A small animated ripple ring round the swimmer, SPD style (recommended; reverses an earlier decision). (b) Keep the straight cut, only make the line shorter and blue.
- **Q7. Where should the shark appear when it hits the raft?** (a) In the water just south of the raft, drawn in front of the riders (recommended). (b) Just east of the raft.
- **Q8. HUD look?** (a) Dark rounded GBA windows for the HUD, cream paper windows for notes, all in the game palette (recommended). (b) Tan paper windows everywhere. (c) Keep today's look.
- **Q9. How see-through should HUD windows be?** (a) 94%: nearly solid, keeps the current spec wording (recommended). (b) Fully solid (spec change). (c) Keep 75%.
- **Q10. Meters?** (a) Label column plus thin bars, Salud first (recommended; spec change). (b) Keep labels inside the bars with darker fills (no spec change).
- **Q11. Hotbar?** (a) Keep today's slots with small digits, the key number only on the selected slot (recommended). (b) One padded hotbar window with the item name and weight as tabs (spec change; the refusal notice must move).
- **Q12. The coast cay's watch post?** (a) Draw an open, ruined garita over its shaded patch (recommended). (b) Keep only the shade.
- **Q13. Night?** (a) The fire casts a warm light pool exactly as far as its company bonus reaches, and night gets slightly brighter (recommended). (b) Only a brighter night. (c) Keep as is.
- **Q14. Squalls?** (a) Rain, a faster sea and foam, and a weather label (recommended). (b) Keep the darker tint only.
- **Q15. Outlines?** (a) Write down today's rule: black on characters and pick-ups, dark tones on buildings (recommended). (b) One outline colour for everything.
- **Q16. Menu font?** (a) Fix the word spacing now, choose one pixel font for all text later (recommended). (b) Press Start 2P everywhere (wide; some menu lines no longer fit). (c) Keep as is.
