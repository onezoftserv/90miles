# 90 Millas — Characters

Every NPC in the game, with backstory, where they appear, what they want, what they give, and how they talk. **AI** marks characters whose dialogue is generated by Haiku from a profile; **HW** marks handwritten lines.

Voice notes are for the Chatterbox clones and for the Haiku prompt. Satire targets the system and daily life; no invented quotes from real politicians.

## Important NPCs (AI dialogue)

### Ramón, el pescador — AI

**Age:** 61. **Found on:** any island, docked at a cove with his boat *La Esperanza II*.

**Backstory.** Ramón fished out of Cojímar for forty years, first for the state cooperative, then for himself when the cooperative had no fuel. In 1994 he watched half his neighborhood build rafts on the beach and he sold them rope. He never left; his wife Nereida was sick and would not go. She died in 2019. His son went in 2021 on a raft Ramón helped build and has not called since. Ramón now fishes the cays "for lobster", which is what he tells the patrols. He is really looking for his son, or for news, or for a reason to stop looking.

**What he wants.** Someone to help him pull his traps (his back is gone), and someone to listen. If a player is a *Pescador*, he recognizes the hands and softens faster.

**Quests.**
- *Las nasas:* pull 4 lobster traps from the reef (eels and barracuda). Reward: rope ×3 and a ride to the next island.
- *La foto:* find his son's raft (a wreck on a later island) and bring back the photo nailed inside. Reward: the *Nudo del pescador* keepsake unlock and the fuel he was saving.
- *El trato:* he will take you to the next island for a favor, but *La Esperanza II* holds four and he counts himself. Someone stays or finds another way.

**Changes the ending.** If you bring him the photo, he comes with you on the final crossing and appears in *Todos*. If you took his boat without him, *Los que llegamos* mentions him.

**Voice.** Slow, dry, swears softly and often. Says *mijo* and *compadre*. Every story starts "En el noventa y cuatro…". Hates the word *bloqueo* and will argue it with anyone. At *El pilote* (final sandbar) without Tomás: "Antes, con un pie aquí arriba ya eras americano." — "¿Y ahora?" — "Ahora eres un tipo parado en un pilote." Haiku profile: `backend/prompts/npcs/ramon.md`.

### La Vieja Caridad, la del faro — AI

**Age:** 78. **Found on:** the lighthouse key (any chain, once per run).

**Backstory.** Caridad was a navigation teacher at the naval academy in Mariel, the only woman in her year. She taught cadets to read stars for twenty years, then was pushed out after her brother left in the Mariel boatlift in 1980 and she refused to denounce him. She came to the lighthouse "temporarily" in 1991. The lighthouse has not worked since the Special Period; she lights it by hand with a kerosene lamp on nights when she sees rafts. The patrols know she is there and leave her alone; one of them was her student.

**What she wants.** Kerosene, news, and someone who will learn properly. She tests you: she will not give the charts to someone who cannot find Polaris.

**Quests.**
- *La lección:* find Polaris on three nights (she asks you to point; a mini-game). Reward: Navigation skill +2 and the charts.
- *El queroseno:* bring 2 fuel jugs so she can light the lamp. Reward: on the final crossing the lighthouse beam shows you the way through the Straits.
- *La carta:* she asks you to carry a letter to her brother in Miami. He has been dead since 2003. Whether you tell her is a choice; both answers are written.

**Changes the ending.** If you lit the lamp, the final crossing shows her beam and she is in the credits of every ending. If a *Maestro* is in the party, she gives the *Medalla de la Caridad* keepsake.

**Voice.** Formal, precise, teacher's cadence. Corrects your grammar and then your knots. Never swears, but says *¡Mira que tú eres bruto!* with love. Quotes Martí and then says Martí would have left too. Haiku profile: `backend/prompts/npcs/caridad.md`.

### Yunior — AI

**Age:** 12. **Found on:** any island after the first, alone, hiding.

**Backstory.** Yunior's mother put him on a raft with his uncle in Santa Cruz del Norte. The raft split in a squall on the first night; he swam to a cay and has been there eleven days eating sea grapes and crabs he cannot cook. His uncle is gone. He has a plastic bag with a passport-sized photo of his mother, a school pin, and a dead phone with a cracked screen that he keeps "charging" in the sun. He is not sad on the outside. He talks constantly, mostly about Messi and about how his uncle will come back.

**What he wants.** To not be left. He attaches to whoever feeds him first.

**Quests.**
- *El teléfono:* find a solar charger (wreck loot). He calls his mother on the final island. Whether the call connects depends on the ending.
- *Tío:* he asks you to look for his uncle. The uncle's body is on a later island. Players can lie or tell him.

**Mechanics.** He eats half rations, cannot swim far, and panics near sharks (morale −10 to everyone). He is small enough to ride the turtle with one adult. He fits anywhere.

**Changes the ending.** If Yunior reaches land, the secret ending *El Niño* plays. If he dies, every ending is colder; his death does not select an ending (*Solo* plays only when exactly one person survives).

**Voice.** Fast, loud, Havana kid slang (*asere, qué volá, candela, tremendo*). Swears, then apologizes, then swears again. Explains the offside rule to sharks. Haiku profile: `backend/prompts/npcs/yunior.md`.

### El Capitán Rodríguez — AI (as antagonist)

**Age:** 44. **Found on:** the guardacostas cutter *Tiburón 3*, early chains. Speaks through a loudhailer and, if you are captured, face to face.

**Backstory.** Rodríguez grew up in Baracoa, joined the coast guard because it fed his family, and believes about half of what he says over the loudhailer. He has pulled drowned children out of the water and has also pulled living ones back to shore to be processed. His own cousin left in 2022. He has Caridad's old navigation textbook on his bridge; he was her student. He is not a cartoon. He is tired, and he wants his shift to end without anyone dying.

**What he wants.** Quiet. Numbers that look good. To not have to decide.

**Encounters.**
- *El aviso:* the loudhailer gives you one warning: go back and nothing happens. Nobody goes back.
- *La captura:* if caught, he interrogates you on the deck. A persuasive player (high morale, *Maestro* or *Médico*) can talk him into "losing" you at the next cay. A low-morale party is returned to the previous island.
- *El trato del capitán:* on a later island, if Caridad's letter is in your inventory, he recognizes the handwriting and lets you pass once.

**Voice.** Official Spanish over the loudhailer, Baracoa Spanish in person. Slogans he does not believe, delivered flat. Haiku profile: `backend/prompts/npcs/rodriguez.md`.

### Rescued balseros (companions, AI)

Rescued balseros join the party as AI companions. Each one has a need, a skill, and a line in the ending. Eight exist; one to three appear per run, rolled from chains not yet used for a companion, so a run with Marisol and a run with Tomás play very differently.

#### Dayana — AI

**Age:** 29. Nurse from Holguín, left because her salary bought eight eggs. Found on a reef island with a broken ankle, splinting it herself. **Skill:** heals the party (+health per night). **Need:** cannot walk fast until healed with aloe + bandage; needs to be carried on crossings. **Ending:** in *Todos* she is the one who finds a phone and calls everyone's family. **Voice:** blunt, clinical, funny about blood; swears at wounds, not people.

#### Papo — AI

**Age:** 52. Former mechanic for the state bus company; built three rafts for other people before building his own. Found alone on a wreck island, repairing the boat you need. **Skill:** builds and repairs twice as fast. **Need:** drinks; has a flask and a thirst meter that drains faster. Morale crashes if the flask is empty. **Ending:** in *Los que llegamos* he is the empty chair if lost. **Voice:** tells jokes about the Chevy he kept alive for 40 years; every object is "a thing of nothing, mijo, give me ten minutes."

#### El Chino — AI

**Age:** 34. Not Chinese; from Chinatown in Havana, nickname since school. Sold black-market internet cards (*las tarjetas*) and knows every slogan ironically. Found on a mangrove island hiding from a patrol. **Skill:** stealth; patrols take longer to spot the party. **Need:** paranoid; refuses fire at night, which fights the party's heat and morale needs. **Ending:** if saved, he narrates the *Todos* ending credits. **Voice:** fastest talker in the game, ironic slogans, calls everyone *socio*.

#### Marisol — AI

**Age:** 24. Seven months pregnant, from Cienfuegos; her husband was on the raft and did not surface. Found on a sandbar under a palm, rationing one coconut a day. **Skill:** none while pregnant, but every NPC in the game softens toward a party with her (Rodríguez releases a party with Marisol on the first capture, once). **Need:** thirst ×1.5, cannot swim at all, cannot climb; must ride a raft, boat or almendrón (never the board, which dumps its rider). Morale −15 to everyone if she is left behind. **Ending:** in *Todos* the credits say the baby's name. **Voice:** quiet, very funny when she decides to be, calls the baby *el polizón* (the stowaway).

#### Doctor Alfredo — AI

**Age:** 67. Left in 1994, lived in Hialeah for 25 years, came back in 2019 to bury his mother and could not get out again when the borders closed. Found on the lighthouse key arguing with Caridad about Martí. **Skill:** full Médico (identifies every plant, heals 2× Dayana's rate, reads expired medicine). **Need:** heart condition: cannot run or swim; needs a pill each day from the expired-medicine pool or health −20 per day. **Ending:** in *Los que llegamos* he is the one who speaks at the pier if he made it; if he died, his bag of pills is on the empty chair. **Voice:** Spanglish; says "en Hialeah" every third sentence; swears in English, prays in Spanish.

#### Yusnavy — AI

**Age:** 31. Left-handed pitcher, had a tryout with a Dominican academy and never got the visa. Found on the rocky key throwing stones at gulls with frightening accuracy. **Skill:** thrown weapons do 3× damage (rocks, coconuts, the baseball); throws rope to a swimmer 10 tiles out. **Need:** his arm: swimming more than 20 tiles injures it (−skill until rested a night). **Ending:** if he reaches land, the *Pelota firmada* keepsake unlocks for everyone in the party. **Voice:** counts everything in innings; describes sharks as batters.

#### Tania, la bloguera — AI

**Age:** 27. Wrote a blog about the queues (*las colas*) and the blackouts until someone knocked on her door. Found hiding in the observation post on the coast cay, writing on the walls. **Skill:** finds found texts at 2× rate; reads the patrol logbook and predicts the next loop. **Need:** she will not be captured: if a squad lands, she runs into the water (sharks). **Ending:** if she makes it, *El Chino* and Tania narrate the credits together. If she dies, her last wall text is the final screen. **Voice:** fast, precise, every sentence has a hashtag she says out loud.

#### El Viejo Tomás — AI

**Age:** 80. Was on a raft in 1994 and was returned; tried again in 2005 and was returned; this is the third time. Found on the shipwreck reef, sitting in the hull camp like it is his living room. **Skill:** knows every chain: reveals all routes on arrival at any island he has been to (all of them). **Need:** walks at half speed; thirst ×1.3; refuses rafts and boards (he has seen three break: "Ni muerto.") and will only cross by boat, almendrón, turtle, or dolphins. The car is different: "¿En almendrón? Eso es otra cosa: los carros americanos no se hunden, los hunden." **Ending:** he does not want to reach land: on the final approach he asks to stay on the sandbar. The party can insist (morale −20, he comes) or let him (he waves from the sandbar in every ending). **Voice:** the slowest speaker; every story starts with a year; swears like a sailor and apologizes to Marisol's belly. **The piling.** If the party reaches *El pilote* on the final sandbar, he remembers the old rule and has plans for it: "Antes era tocar tierra y ya. Pies secos." A younger party member: "Abuelo, eso se acabó en el 2017." He: "¿Y entonces pa' qué me apuran? Yo me quedo en mi pilote." It fits his wish to stay on the sandbar.

## Animal companions

### Vieja, the loggerhead turtle

Sixty years old, three fishing nets' worth of scars. Found tangled in a ghost net at low tide on reef islands. Cut her free (knife, 30 s, sharks arrive at 20 s) and feed her 3 fish over two days; she then follows the party and can carry two across a crossing. Frightened by searchlights and shark attacks: she dives, dumping riders. Found again on later islands if treated well.

### The dolphin pod

Six spotted dolphins. Appear at dawn in clear weather, more often if a *Músico* plays. Swimming inside the pod keeps sharks away for one crossing. Cannot be fed or kept.
